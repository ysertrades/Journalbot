Questions

Good to know.

Who can open a server's journal?

The same people who can use /journal: the server's allowed users, or anyone with Manage Server when no list is set. Settings always need Manage Server.

Which brokers does the CSV import read?

Anything with a symbol and a PnL column: Hyperprop, Tradovate, TopstepX, NinjaTrader exports and plain sheets. Side, size, prices, times, fees and hold time are read when present.

Why did my 7 PM trade land on tomorrow?

Futures sessions open at 6 PM New York time, so evening trades belong to the next trading day. You can switch to a forex session or the plain calendar in Settings.

Can I put the panel inside Whop?

Yes. Allow Whop as a frame ancestor and add the panel's address as an app view. Sign-in opens in a small window and comes straight back.

Is my data shared anywhere?

No. Everything stays in the bot's own database and your Discord forums. Discord login only reads your name and the servers you share with the bot.
